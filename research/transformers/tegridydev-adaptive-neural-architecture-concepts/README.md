# Adaptive Neural Architectures: Experiments and Questions

This overview organises eleven adaptive architecture leads by the object that changes: retained memory, feature influence, routing, representation or stored precision.

[Read the study](tegridydev-adaptive-neural-architecture-concepts.md)

The article distinguishes proposed mechanisms, illustrative calculations and the work still needed for an experimental result.

## Current scope

The individual mechanisms have supporting implementations: [Temporal Attention for Irregular Observations](../temporal-attention-that-changes-predictions/README.md), [Self Healing Tokens: Selective Denoising Experiments](../self-healing-tokens/README.md), [Face Based Attention: Gated Feature Mixing](../face-based-attention-circuits/README.md), [Hydraform: Adaptive Attention Head Mutation](../hydraform/README.md) and [Transformer Memory: Causal Retrieval and Adaptation](../reflective-transformer-memory-and-adaptation/README.md). The five seed comparisons do not establish a benefit from combining these mechanisms: FBAC shows no consistent advantage over its controls, majority repair beats the learned models, guided mutation does not beat the fixed/reset controls, and temporal gains reverse under a timing shift. Geometric, phase and hybrid combinations remain proposals requiring independent evaluation.
